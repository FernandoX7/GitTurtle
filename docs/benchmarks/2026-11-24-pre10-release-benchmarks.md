## 6. Headless GPUI UI-Thread & Accessibility Benchmarks (`--release`)

- **`quiet_landing`:** UI-thread handling of an already-answered quiet refresh reply through `run_until_parked`, including any redraws (`0` frames drawn when unchanged).

- **`github_review_select`:** UI-thread `select_review_file` handler call only (excludes GPUI effect flush, rendering, and background preparation).
- **`a11y_text_runs`:** `InputState::accessible_text`, `AccessibleText::write`, `TreeUpdate`, and `accesskit_atspi_common::Adapter::update` (excludes D-Bus IPC and layout, prepaint, or paint).
