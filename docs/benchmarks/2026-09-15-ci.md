## Measurement boundary

Elapsed means workflow creation to the last job completion. Execution span means
first job start to last job completion; it is an observed span, not a computed
job-dependency critical path. Runner time sums job intervals, including overlap;
it is neither elapsed time nor billed minutes. Queue means creation to first job
start. Each named run is one observation, with different source, coverage and
queue conditions. These samples do not establish a performance distribution or p95.

## Cache, security and remaining evidence

All four restores in the recorded product run missed: debug/release respectively
0.848s/0.657s on Linux and 0.989s/1.299s on macOS. These short miss checks do not
measure cache transfer benefit. PR lanes did not save caches; retained compressed
size, save overhead and warm build reuse remain unavailable. Reviewed integration
into `main`, a successful eligible main cache save and comparable restored runs
on both platforms are needed for warm measurements.

Historical [CodeQL 34992342508](https://github.com/FernandoX7/GitTurtle/actions/runs/34992342508)
consumed **25m19s** of runner time. The baseline Quality+CodeQL path spanned
**32m43s** from earliest creation to final job completion, totaling **83m19s**
of runner time. Older notes using run-update timestamps give 32m40s for Quality
and 32m44s combined; the one-second difference is the measurement boundary.
CodeQL was later explicitly retired by the maintainer in favor of independent
security review. Its removal changes coverage and cannot be counted as an
analysis optimization. Current required-path observations include Quality and its
compatibility checks; they do not assert a security review occurred.

A separate [icon-only diagnostic 35021732905](https://github.com/FernandoX7/GitTurtle/actions/runs/35021732905)
