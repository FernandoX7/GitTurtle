# Community conduct

GitTurtle welcomes people of every background and experience level. Help make
issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces useful and respectful.

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Give specific, constructive
  feedback and leave room for different experience and communication styles.
- Respect people's identities, boundaries, and time. Harassment, discriminatory
  remarks, threats, sexualized conduct, and repeated unwanted contact are not
  acceptable.
- Do not publish anyone's private information, credentials, or repository
  contents without permission. Do not use project spaces for spam.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct misleading information, and follow reasonable
  moderation requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, or restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Repeated or serious conduct
can lead to removal from project spaces. These expectations apply to maintainers
as well as contributors.

Use GitHub's **Report content** menu or
[report abuse to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) for
conduct concerns. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. GitTurtle has
no separate confidential maintainer contact for conduct reports; GitHub's
security advisory channel is reserved for software vulnerabilities.
