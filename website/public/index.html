Performance

More worktrees.
Less waiting.

GitTurtle started with Fernando’s own projects: 60 GB across many worktrees. Waiting for repositories to load and commits to open kept breaking his flow. He built GitTurtle to make those everyday moments feel quick again.

See the measurements →

Window on screen

115 ms

Change your Omarchy theme and GitTurtle follows in the open window. It picks up your monospace font, works tiled or floating on Wayland, and opens your repository in your preferred editor or terminal.
