Performance

More worktrees.
Less waiting.

GitTurtle started with Fernando’s own projects: 60 GB across many worktrees. Waiting for repositories to load and commits to open kept breaking his flow. He built GitTurtle to make those everyday moments feel quick again.

See the measurements →

First-class Omarchy support

Your desktop.
Your colors.

Change your Omarchy 3.x or 4.x theme and GitTurtle follows in the open window. It picks up your monospace font, supports Wayland single-instance handoff and middle-click selection, works tiled or floating, and opens your preferred editor or terminal.
